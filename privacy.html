Privacy

This site collects very little, and we say exactly what. Here is what happens to anything you give us, in plain words.

In short

  • The only thing you can give us is the interest form. It goes to our email and nowhere else.
  • We use it to reply to you and to tell you when workshops launch. We never sell it or share it.
  • Visits are counted without cookies and without storing your IP address.
  • The activities and manuals ask for nothing. Finished activities are remembered on your own device only.
  • The form is for adults. Students ask a parent, teacher or counselor to sign up for them.

What the interest form collects

When you join the interest list we ask for:

  • Your name and email address, so we can reply and send launch updates.
  • Your school or organization, which is optional.
  • Your role (for example counselor, youth leader, parent), what you are interested in, and which format fits you best, so we know what to plan.
  • A message, if you want to leave one. Optional.
  • A checkbox confirming you are 18 or older and signing up as an adult contact.

The form is handled by Formspree, a form service. Formspree passes your answers to us by email and keeps a copy in our Formspree inbox so nothing gets lost. We do not add tracking to the form, and the honeypot field that catches spam bots is never shown to people.

What we do with it

  • Reply to you if you asked something.
  • Email you when workshops launch, when a new manual is ready, or when a plan changes in a way that matters to you.
  • Count how many schools, organizations and parents are interested, so we can decide what to build first. We only ever share those numbers as totals, never names.

We do not sell your information, trade it, or give it to anyone to market to you. Only the three of us on the Own Two Feet team can read the inbox.

Want out? Email hello.owntwofeet@gmail.com and say so, or reply to any email from us. We delete you from our inbox and from Formspree, usually within a few days.

How visits are counted

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It records which page was opened, which site sent you here if any, your screen size, your browser type, your browser language and your country. It sets no cookies and does not store your IP address. We use the counts to see which pages help and whether people who visit also join the list.

GoatCounter does not count visits when the site is opened from a file on your computer.

What stays on your device

When you finish an activity (Tool Time, Saturday on $25, Scam or Safe, Make the Call or Laundry Sort-Out), your browser remembers that with a small note stored on your device, so the Done mark survives a reload. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

The activities and manuals never ask for your name, email or anything else.

Children

Own Two Feet workshops are for students in grades 3 to 8, and the activities and manuals on this site are written for them. But the interest form is for adults only, and it asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If a child has sent us something, a parent or guardian can email hello.owntwofeet@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Other services the site uses

Loading a page on this site also talks to a few services that are standard for websites. Each one can see the normal details of a web request, like your IP address and browser type, and has its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the small library that draws the 3D tools on the home page and in Tool Time.

We do not use advertising networks, social media buttons, or any tracker beyond the visit counter above.

Changes

If any of this changes, we update this page and the date below. Big changes, like a new service that handles your information, get a line in our next email too.
